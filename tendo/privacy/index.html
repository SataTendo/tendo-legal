Privacy Policy — Tendo

This Privacy Policy describes how Tendo (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use the Tendo iOS application.

1. Overview

Tendo is a habit tracking and focus timer app with optional community features (rooms, templates, and squads), weekly cards, and consumable in-app purchases called Sparks. We design features so you can use core tools and community controls from within the app. You can contact us at satatendo@gmail.com.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use Tendo, we may process:

  • Session and preference data such as whether you have an active session, community alias, focus defaults, and feature toggles you set in Settings.
  • Habit and focus records you create (names, schedules, check-ins, session timings, weekly statistics).
  • Media you attach such as habit cover photos (camera or photo library) and optional voice notes (and optional speech transcription text when you choose Transcribe).
  • Sparks wallet and purchase records needed to credit consumable purchases verified through Apple.
  • Community overlays such as likes, reports, blocks, published templates you create, and squad membership state associated with your community profile on this device.
  • Device identifiers Apple provides for StoreKit as part of purchase verification (we do not receive your full payment card details).

We do not sell personal information.

4. Facial data

4.1 What is collected

Tendo does not collect biometric faceprints, face templates, or Face ID data. If you choose to take or select a habit cover photo, that photo may depict a face or other people. That image is treated as a user-provided photo, not as biometric facial recognition data.

4.2 Planned use

Cover photos are used only to display habit covers and related previews inside Tendo. Voice notes are used only for playback and optional transcription you trigger. We do not use photos or voice notes to identify people across apps or to build advertising profiles.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and storage

Cover photos and voice notes you attach are stored in the app’s sandboxed file storage on your device for display and playback. We do not upload habit cover photos or voice notes to our servers for facial analysis. Purchase verification is handled by Apple StoreKit. Report emails you send via the mail composer leave your device through your mail provider.

4.4 Retention

User-attached media remains until you delete the attachment, delete the related habit, use Clear All Data, or remove the app. After Clear All Data or uninstall, user-owned media and records described in this policy are removed from the app’s storage on that device.

4.5 Scope of this chapter

This chapter (Section 4) is the explanation of collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention of facial-related content for Guideline 2.1 / 5.1.1 review purposes: Tendo does not collect biometric face data; optional user photos may depict a face and are stored for in-app cover display as described above.

5. Third-party AI

Tendo does not send your habit photos, voice notes, prompts, or personal content to a third-party AI API for generation or analysis. Optional speech transcription uses Apple’s on-device / system Speech framework when available and when you tap Transcribe.

6. In-app purchases (Sparks)

Sparks are consumable virtual items purchased through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Apple processes payment. We do not store your credit card number. Sparks balance and related purchase bookkeeping are kept with the app on your device. Sparks are used for premium weekly card save/export, publishing templates, and creating squads. Purchases cannot be restored as Apple consumables; if you use Clear All Data or delete the app, Sparks on that device are lost. Refund requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies.

7. Community, reports, and UGC safety

Community features may include rooms, templates, and squads with user-generated or seed content. Tendo provides keyword filtering before publish, report and block controls, and ways to remove your own published items. Reports can open an email draft to satatendo@gmail.com with a content snippet so we can review. We aim to act on valid reports of objectionable content or abusive users within 24 hours (including removing content and ejecting offending authors from the community experience where applicable). Community age eligibility for anonymous posting contexts is 18+.

8. Session controls and deletion

  • Get Started creates a local session so you can use the app.
  • End Session ends the active session while keeping stored credentials/preferences as designed; the next cold start may show Get Started again.
  • Delete Community Account removes community profile overlays (published templates, likes, blocks, reports, squad participation state) while leaving core habit/focus tools and Sparks unless you also clear all data.
  • Clear All Data permanently deletes user-owned data on this device (habits you created, overlays, Sparks balance and purchase bookkeeping, media you attached, and related preferences). Built-in catalog/seed content may remain as part of the app binary.

9. Children

Tendo is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact satatendo@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
