How we help

Thanks for using Tendo. This page covers common questions about habits, focus sessions, Sparks, community tools, and data controls. For account-sensitive or purchase issues handled by Apple, we may ask you to use Apple’s support flows as well.

Contact

To report inappropriate community content, use in-app Report controls or email us with the content details. You can also open Contact Developer / Support from Settings.

Common topics

Getting started and sessions

  • After Get Started, you land on Today.
  • End Session ends the current session; the next cold start may show Get Started again.
  • Agree to the Terms checkbox when shown before continuing.

Habits and focus

  • Create or edit habits from Today (including cover photo and optional voice note).
  • Start focus from Today or Focus Setup; pause/end from Focus Session; review rounds in Summary.
  • Weekly stats and card export live under Week / Card Studio.

Permissions

  • Notifications are requested the first time you open Today. Tendo sends notifications related to features you use; you can disable them in iOS Settings.
  • Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Speech are requested when you use the related habit media features.

Sparks purchases

  • Open Get Sparks from Settings (or when a paid action needs more balance).
  • Sparks are Apple consumable In-App Purchases. They are stored with the app on your device and cannot be restored like non-consumables.
  • Spending: premium weekly card save/export (1), publish template (1), create squad (2).
  • For billing refunds, contact Apple Support / report a problem in your purchase history.

Community

  • Toggle Community in Settings. When off, the Community tab is hidden.
  • Templates support Like, Report, Block, Import, and Publish (costs Sparks).
  • Squads support create/join style flows and member safety actions.

Clear All Data / Delete Community Account

  • Delete Community Account removes community profile overlays while keeping tool data and Sparks unless you also clear everything.
  • Clear All Data permanently deletes user-owned data on this device, including Sparks. This cannot be undone.
